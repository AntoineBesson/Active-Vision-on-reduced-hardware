# Related work ledger: what to cite and how we differ

One entry per paper that a reviewer could put next to this study. The TRIES
ledgers record what we tried; this file records what others published. Add an
entry whenever a paper is found, even a damaging one.

**Our position, to hold against every entry.** The backbone is frozen
(PIDNet-S, BiSeNetV2, public Cityscapes weights): nothing is trained through
it. Every method is judged against the *same model on the whole frame at a
lower scale*, at equal measured latency on a T4 (mean and p95), on video
(Cityscapes-VPS). Negative results are reported. Most papers below train the
network with their module and compare with a slower network or with FLOPs.

**Status of each entry** (column "Checked"):
`abstract` = title, venue and abstract confirmed by a web search on the date
given; `memory` = written from memory, to confirm before citing; `read` = the
paper itself was read. No entry is `read` yet, so **no number from these
papers may be quoted** until it is.

| Thavamani, Li, Ferroni, Ramanan. **Learning to Zoom and Unzoom.** CVPR 2023. arXiv 2303.15390 | Zoom the input with a separable saliency warp, run the network, unzoom the features after the FPN with a piecewise bilinear inverse. Detection, **semantic segmentation on Cityscapes**, 3D detection. For segmentation: PSPNet-R50-FPN trained with the warp under the uniform baseline's settings (MMSeg defaults; batch 16, 80k iterations per the appendix); **fixed saliency only** (the mean of the ground-truth semantic boundaries of train), no adaptive or temporal saliency, no video; inputs are a **centred square crop** resized to 64, 128, 256 or 512 px. Per class at 512: rider −6.7, pole −1.8, truck +4.9. Accuracy–cost plot in FLOPs (Fig. 7), not latency; no magnification above native resolution for segmentation. Authors' limits: unzooming features at small scales is destructive; fixed saliency loses at the top and bottom of the frame (appendix, from an extracted summary). | **The closest prior work, and the one a fine-tuned warp (Method 11) would most nearly repeat.** What they did not do, and Method 11 must: (1) fine-tune public *real-time* segmenters (PIDNet-S, BiSeNetV2) on the full 1024 × 2048 frame, not train PSPNet on square crops; (2) judge at equal *measured* T4 latency against the envelope of every model's full-frame curve, including the same model fine-tuned on uniform frames with the same budget; (3) on video (Cityscapes-VPS) with tiny/small moving-object recall, where their per-class table already shows riders and poles losing at 512; (4) scale 1 with magnification; (5) a prior fitted on the model's own errors (Method 5's P) as well as their boundary prior. ## Crops and glimpses over a cheap context (Method 2, and the glimpses of Method 4)

| Paper | What they do | Difference to state | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chai. **Patchwork: A Patch-wise Attention Network for Efficient Object Detection and Segmentation in Video Streams.** ICCV 2019. arXiv 1904.01784 | Processes only a small window of each frame, memory cells carry context from past frames, a Q-learned policy picks the next window. ImageNet VID, DAVIS. | **The closest prior work to Method 4** (window + memory + learned policy). Trained end to end, object-centric tasks. Our Method 4 bound says that with a frozen real-time segmenter on Cityscapes-VPS even an oracle window selector with a canvas stays below the full frame at equal latency. To read: their baseline (is it the same network at lower resolution?) and latency accounting. | abstract, 2026-11-16 |
| Chen, Jiang, Wang, Cui, Qian. **Collaborative Global-Local Networks for Memory-Efficient Segmentation of Ultra-High Resolution Images (GLNet).** CVPR 2019. arXiv 1905.06368 | A global branch on the downscaled image and a local branch on full-resolution crops, fused. Aerial and medical images of up to 30 M pixels. | Same context + crops structure as Method 2, but trained, on images far larger than 2 MP, and the goal is memory. Supports our reading that crops pay only when the full frame cannot be run at all. | abstract, 2026-11-16 |
| Mnih, Heess, Graves, Kavukcuoglu. **Recurrent Models of Visual Attention.** NeurIPS 2014 | Glimpse network with a recurrent state and a policy trained by reinforcement. | Origin of glimpse-based active vision; classification, trained. Cite as the idea, not as a baseline. | memory |
| Seifi, Tuytelaars. **Attend and Segment: Attention Guided Active Semantic Segmentation.** ECCV 2020. arXiv 2007.11548 | Up to 10 retina-like 48 px glimpses (3 scales) on a 128 × 256 frame, next glimpse at the lowest predicted certainty, a small U-Net trained end to end (no RL); labels every pixel, seen or not. **Cityscapes**, CamVid, KITTI. | **Evidence for our Methods 2 and 4.** Their own scale-only table on Cityscapes: the whole frame at 1/16 of the pixels (6.25 %) reaches 78.9 % pixel accuracy, above the 10-glimpse agent at 18 % of the pixels (78.1 %). Metric is pixel accuracy (mIoU only per class: 0.508 vs 0.590 for the full 128 × 256 U-Net); compute is counted in pixels, not latency. | read (extracted text), 2026-11-25 |
| Hassler, Henrich, Berreby, Zhu, Ecker. **FoAViS: Analyzing Learned Fixation Strategies in a Foveated Active Vision System.** arXiv 2610.41987 (2026, venue unknown) | Log-polar 64 × 64 glimpses, 4 fixations, a policy trained by policy gradient, an LSTM spatial memory, backbone and heads trained. ImageNet, ADE20K, COCO-Stuff; no Cityscapes, no video. | A trained version of our Method 4 (glimpses + memory + learned policy). Their learned glimpses lose to a passive model on the whole image at the same pixel budget (ADE20K −6.4 mIoU, COCO-Stuff −4.8) and beat random fixations by +2.5 / +1.7: the same ordering we find with a frozen model. No latency. | read (extracted text, appendix not read), 2026-11-25 |
| Caccavella, Fra, Ziegler, D'Angelo, Sandamirskaya. **Efficient Semantic Understanding from Digital Foveation.** arXiv 2609.04088 (2026, venue unknown) | A low-resolution copy gives saliency and a context embedding; fixations by winner-take-all with inhibition of return (training-free); foveal crops segmented and written into a semantic canvas with Gaussian weights. CLEVR, ADE20K objects only; no Cityscapes, no video. | **Closest published design to our Method 4** (saliency-driven crops into a persistent canvas). Judged in FLOPs against Lite R-ASPP on the whole image: 98 % of its object recall at 87 % of its FLOPs, but full-canvas mIoU 0.165 vs 0.376. Our bound adds measured latency and the same model at a lower scale. | read (extracted text), 2026-11-25 |
| Pardyl, Wronka, Wołczyk, Adamczewski, Trzciński, Zieliński. **AdaGlimpse: Active Visual Exploration with Arbitrary Glimpse Position and Scale.** ECCV 2024. arXiv 2404.03482 | Glimpses of any position and scale chosen by Soft Actor-Critic; ViT-B pretrained on random glimpses, fine-tuned. ImageNet, COCO, SUN360, ADE20K; no Cityscapes. | Exploration under a pixel budget, no full-frame low-resolution baseline. Shows what a trained glimpse policy costs (RL and backbone epochs alternated over 100 epochs). | read (extracted text), 2026-11-25 |

| Xu, Fu, Yang, Lee. **Dynamic Video Segmentation Network (DVSNet).** CVPR 2018. arXiv 1804.00931 | Splits the frame into regions; a decision network sends each region either to the segmentation network or to a flow network that warps the previous result. Cityscapes. | **The closest prior work to Method 3** (per-region recompute or reuse). They warp with learned optical flow and train a decision network, on a heavy segmenter; we gate with a camera-motion homography and a fixed rule, on real-time segmenters, where the full frame is already cheap. Our negative result is about that regime. | abstract, 2026-11-16 |

| Shelhamer, Rakelly, Hoffman, Darrell. **Clockwork Convnets for Video Semantic Segmentation.** ECCV 2016 Workshops | Updates deep layers less often than shallow ones, on a fixed or adaptive clock. | Layer-wise reuse in time; needs access inside the network. ## To search next

- Video segmentation with an explicit persistent memory or canvas (for Method 4), beyond Patchwork.
- Foveated or log-polar sampling for segmentation, and any work testing warps on a **frozen** network (the direct competitor of Method 5's framing). Searched 2026-11-25: FoAViS and Digital Foveation found (above), both trained, neither on Cityscapes; still no paper on a frozen real-time segmenter.
- Studies that compare efficiency methods against the same model at lower resolution at equal measured latency (support for our evaluation rule).
- Per-object or motion-aware recall metrics for driving scenes (for the moving-object metric).
